# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-05

- First public version.
- `skills/vc-diligence/SKILL.md`: the pipeline as a Claude skill, with triggers, procedure and quality gates.
- `templates/`: document register, per-document verbatim, extract and BLUF, master synthesis, thesis memo, asks list.

- `examples/larchwick-secondary/`: synthetic nine-document data room with three planted contradictions and the expected outputs.
- `tests/`: 54 unittest tests, no network.
