# D09 - Right of first refusal and co-sale agreement (BLUF)

1. **What:** executed ROFR and co-sale agreement, 2026-03-02. It sets the clock on any sale of the block.
2. **Key facts:** company ROFR 15 days, investors 10 more, over-allotment and co-sale 5 more, ROFR closing by day 35, sale to us by day 45. Board approval in its sole discretion.
3. **Price impact:** our signed price becomes the price at which the company or any investor, including the Strategic Investor, can take the block.
4. **Conflicts:** the seller's 10-day timeline cannot be met without written waivers.
5. **Ask:** written waivers from the company and investors, and board approval, before we spend on documents.
