## Recitals (parties)
> The Investors listed on Schedule A, the Key Holders listed on Schedule B, and each holder of Common Stock listed on Schedule C (the "Common Holders", together with the Investors and the Key Holders, the "Stockholders").

Schedule C lists the seller as a Common Holder.

## §1.2 Board composition
> The size of the Board shall be five directors: (a) one person designated by the holders of a majority of the Series B Preferred Stock; (b) one person designated by the holders of a majority of the Series A Preferred Stock; (c) two persons designated by the Key Holders; and (d) one independent person approved by the other directors.

## §3.1 Drag-along
> If (a) the Board, (b) the holders of a majority of the outstanding Preferred Stock, voting together as a single class on an as-converted basis, and (c) the holders of a majority of the Common Stock then held by the Key Holders who are then providing services to the Company, each approve a Sale of the Company, then each Stockholder shall vote in favor of the Sale of the Company and, if the sale is structured as a sale of stock, shall sell its Shares on the terms approved.

## §3.2 Conditions
> Each holder of a class or series of capital stock shall receive the same form and amount of consideration per share as each other holder of that class or series, after giving effect to the liquidation preferences set forth in the Restated Certificate.

## §4.2 Proxy
> Each Stockholder hereby appoints the Chief Executive Officer of the Company, and any designee of the Chief Executive Officer, as attorney and proxy to vote all of such Stockholder's Shares as required by §1 and §3 if such Stockholder fails to vote as so required. The proxy is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

## §7.1 Additional parties
> As a condition to any Transfer of Shares, the transferee shall execute a joinder to this Agreement and shall be bound as a Stockholder of the same type as the transferor. Any Transfer made without such joinder shall be void.
