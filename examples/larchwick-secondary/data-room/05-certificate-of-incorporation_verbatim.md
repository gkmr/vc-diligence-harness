## Art. IV(A) Authorized stock
> The total number of shares of all classes of stock which the Corporation shall have authority to issue is 39,500,000 shares, consisting of (i) 30,000,000 shares of Common Stock and (ii) 9,500,000 shares of Preferred Stock, of which 3,000,000 shares are designated Series Seed Preferred Stock, 3,500,000 shares are designated Series A Preferred Stock and 3,000,000 shares are designated Series B Preferred Stock.

## Art. IV(B) §1.1 Preferential payments to holders of Series B Preferred Stock
> In the event of any liquidation, dissolution or winding up of the Corporation or any Deemed Liquidation Event, the holders of shares of Series B Preferred Stock then outstanding shall be entitled to be paid out of the assets available for distribution, before any payment to the holders of Series A Preferred Stock, Series Seed Preferred Stock or Common Stock, an amount per share equal to the greater of (i) the Series B Original Issue Price, plus any declared but unpaid dividends, or (ii) the amount per share that would have been payable had all shares of Series B Preferred Stock been converted into Common Stock immediately prior to such event. The "Series B Original Issue Price" shall mean $4.20 per share, subject to appropriate adjustment in the event of any stock dividend, stock split, combination or other similar recapitalization.

## Art. IV(B) §1.2 Series A and Series Seed
> After payment in full to the holders of Series B Preferred Stock, the holders of Series A Preferred Stock and Series Seed Preferred Stock shall be paid, on a pari passu basis, an amount per share equal to the greater of (i) the applicable Original Issue Price or (ii) the amount per share payable on conversion into Common Stock. The "Series A Original Issue Price" shall mean $2.00 per share and the "Series Seed Original Issue Price" shall mean $0.50 per share.

## Art. IV(B) §1.3 Remaining assets
> After the payments required by §1.1 and §1.2, the remaining assets available for distribution shall be distributed among the holders of Common Stock pro rata based on the number of shares held.

## Art. IV(B) §3.3 Series B protective provisions
> So long as at least 1,000,000 shares of Series B Preferred Stock remain outstanding, the Corporation shall not, without the written consent of the holders of a majority of the Series B Preferred Stock, (iv) effect any Deemed Liquidation Event in which the holders of Series B Preferred Stock would receive less than 1.0x the Series B Original Issue Price per share.

## Art. IV(B) §4.1 Conversion
> Each share of Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into the number of shares of Common Stock determined by dividing the applicable Original Issue Price by the applicable Conversion Price. The initial Conversion Price for each series equals its Original Issue Price.
