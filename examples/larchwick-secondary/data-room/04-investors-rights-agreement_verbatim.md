## §1.1 Definitions
> "Major Investor" means any Investor that, individually or together with such Investor's Affiliates, holds at least 750,000 shares of Registrable Securities (as adjusted for any stock split, stock dividend, combination or recapitalization).

> "Registrable Securities" means (i) the Common Stock issuable or issued upon conversion of the Preferred Stock; and (ii) the Common Stock issued or issuable upon exercise of the Warrants; excluding in all cases any Registrable Securities sold to the public.

## §2.1 Demand registration
> If at any time after the earlier of (i) five years after the date of this Agreement or (ii) 180 days after the effective date of the IPO, the Company receives a request from Holders of at least 40% of the Registrable Securities then outstanding, the Company shall file a registration statement covering the Registrable Securities so requested.

## §2.11 Market stand-off
> Each Holder, each holder of 1% or more of the outstanding Common Stock, and each transferee of any of them, shall not sell or otherwise transfer any Common Stock during the period beginning on the date of the final prospectus for the IPO and ending 180 days after that date.

## §3.1 Delivery of financial statements
> The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor, and to any other holder of at least 750,000 shares of Common Stock (as adjusted) who has executed a joinder to this Agreement (each, an "Information Holder"): (a) within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, audited annual financial statements; and (b) within 45 days after the end of each of the first three quarters, unaudited quarterly financial statements.

## §3.3 Confidentiality and competitors
> The Company is not obligated to provide information to any Information Holder that the Board reasonably determines to be a competitor of the Company.

## §3.4 Observer
> The Company shall invite one representative of the Strategic Investor to attend all meetings of the Board in a non-voting observer capacity and shall give the representative copies of all materials provided to directors.
