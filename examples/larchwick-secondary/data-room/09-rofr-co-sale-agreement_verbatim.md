## §2.1 Notice
> Each Selling Holder proposing to make a Transfer of Shares shall deliver a Proposed Transfer Notice to the Company and each Investor before the Transfer. The notice shall state the number of Offered Shares, the price per share, the identity of the Proposed Transferee and all other material terms.

## §2.2 Company right of first refusal
> The Company may purchase all or any part of the Offered Shares on the terms in the Proposed Transfer Notice by notice to the Selling Holder within 15 days after receipt of the Proposed Transfer Notice.

## §2.3 Investor secondary refusal right
> (a) If the Company does not purchase all of the Offered Shares, each Investor may purchase its pro rata share of the remaining Offered Shares by notice within 10 days after the end of the Company's 15-day period.

> (b) Each Investor that elects to purchase its full pro rata share may purchase any shares not taken by other Investors by notice within 5 days after the Company gives notice of the undersubscription.

## §2.5 Closing
> The closing of any purchase of Offered Shares by the Company or the Investors shall take place no later than 35 days after delivery of the Proposed Transfer Notice.

## §2.6 Transfer to the Proposed Transferee
> Offered Shares not purchased by the Company or the Investors may be Transferred to the Proposed Transferee on terms no more favorable than those in the Proposed Transfer Notice, provided the Transfer is completed no later than 45 days after delivery of the Proposed Transfer Notice. Any Transfer after that date requires a new Proposed Transfer Notice.

## §3.1 Co-sale
> Each Investor that does not exercise its rights under §2 may, by notice within the period in §2.3(b), elect to sell to the Proposed Transferee, on the same terms, up to its pro rata share of the Offered Shares, and the number of Offered Shares the Selling Holder may sell shall be reduced accordingly.

## §5.1 Board approval
> No Selling Holder shall Transfer any Shares without the prior written approval of the Board, which the Board may grant or withhold in its sole discretion.

## §5.2 Joinder
> Any Transfer is conditioned on the transferee executing a joinder to this Agreement and to the Voting Agreement.

## §6.1 Exempt Transfers
> §2, §3 and §5.1 do not apply to (a) a Transfer for bona fide estate planning to a trust for the benefit of the Selling Holder or immediate family, (b) a Transfer by an entity to its Affiliates, or (c) a repurchase of Shares by the Company, provided in each case that the transferee executes a joinder.
